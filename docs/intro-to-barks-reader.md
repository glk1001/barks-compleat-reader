<span class="drop-cap">W</span>elcome to ***The Compleat Barks Disney Reader.*** This app is for owners of the digital
*Fantagraphics Complete Carl Barks Disney Library.* I built it to fulfil a lifelong dream: to
read all the Barks Disney stories uncut and in order. The Fantagraphics volumes supplied most
of the source material, but a generic comic reader can't do a library like this justice, so I
wrote my own.

The reader works straight from your own .zip or .cbz files. It centres each page, numbers
pages as they were originally published, and remembers your place — in both the table of
contents and each story — so you can pick up where you left off.

Undoing the censorship mattered most to me. *"Good Deeds"* (WDCS #34) and *"Silent Night"*
are restored, the missing page of *"The Bill Collectors"* (WDCS #74) is back, and all three
are coloured in the flat Fantagraphics style. Dialogue changes have been reverted to the
original US editions, and I've brought back the Harpies in *"The Golden Fleecing"*, the
"Deah" dialect in *"Lost in the Andes!"* and Bombie's blank eyes in *"Voodoo Hoodoo"*. Not
everyone will agree with these choices — if you don't, you can turn each one off in Settings.

I also wanted to show how Barks' stories evolved. You can browse the library by his actual
submission dates and watch his style develop over his quarter-century career. You can also
browse by series, or by tags. And I've OCR'd every narration box and speech bubble in all the
volumes to build a complete Barksian word index: search for any word or phrase, and the
results link to the exact pages.

There's also a built-in Carl Barks Wiki. It has a page for every story, with background
notes, chronologies, and reference tables for dates, publications, and payments — all
cross-linked and searchable. And when a wiki page makes you want to read the story itself,
you can jump straight from the page to the comic.

Finally, the look: Barks panels fill the backgrounds everywhere. I wanted this reader to be
wall-to-wall Barks — a digital home for my favourite artist of the twentieth century.
